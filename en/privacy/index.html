2. Game progress and preferences

The game stores progress, discovered Pibbles, worlds and habitats, coins and treats, inventory, missions, tutorials, songs and records, and language, sound and accessibility preferences on your device. They let you continue playing and apply the game rules.

This version uses local storage. It does not provide a player account or cloud backup. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage may delete your progress.

3. Age and protection of children

The game is intended for ages 12 and up, including 12-year-old players, teens and adults. At the start you may enter your age or choose not to answer. The entered age is used only to determine an age group—under 13, 13–17 or 18 and over—and is then discarded. Only the age group or decision not to answer is saved on the device; we do not keep a date of birth.

In this version ads remain disabled for anyone under 18 or whose age is unknown. Entering an age is not consent to advertising and does not replace any required privacy choice.

A parent or guardian may contact us about information concerning their child that is under our control. Children do not need to send us contact details to play.

4. Ads and privacy choices

The game integrates Unity LevelPlay and the ironSource, Unity Ads and Google AdMob advertising networks. When available, rewarded ads are optional and may grant the stated benefit upon completion. Availability depends on the service configuration and permitted audience.

Advertising providers may process IP addresses and approximate location derived from them, device or app characteristics and identifiers, ad interactions, and performance and diagnostic data. Purposes include ad delivery and measurement, service operation, security and fraud prevention. Personalized advertising, when available, depends on applicable choices and conditions; an adult age selection alone does not grant permission.

We use Google User Messaging Platform to manage privacy choices where required. Where applicable the game displays a form and provides Privacy options in Settings to review your choices. You can also adjust Android advertising settings. Declining personalization does not prevent local play, but may affect the type or availability of ads.

5. Mission time and support

The game makes an HTTPS request to TimeAPI to check the date for daily missions. Our request does not include game progress, an email or a player identifier. The provider may receive an IP address and technical request data as part of the connection.

When you email support, we receive your email address and any messages or files you choose to send. We use these to answer and resolve your support or privacy request, not for marketing. Do not send passwords, access codes or information unrelated to your request.

6. Providers and international processing

These services may process information in countries other than your own, according to their roles, terms and applicable safeguards. We do not upload your game save to a studio account as part of time requests or advertising.

7. Retention and deletion

Progress and preferences remain on your device for continued play. The in-game reset may retain an active subscription and does not cancel subscriptions. Clear the app storage in Android or uninstall the app to remove all local storage. This does not delete records independently retained by providers or cancel Google-managed payments or subscriptions.

We retain support and privacy correspondence for up to 12 months after resolution, unless a legal obligation or dispute requires longer retention. We then remove it from our support systems. External providers apply their own retention policies; we cannot promise to delete records outside our control.

You may request access, correction, deletion or objection regarding data under our control, ask about limiting its use, or withdraw consent where applicable. Email lathortylla@gmail.com with the subject Privacy — Pibble Evolution, a reply address, a description of the request and enough information to locate relevant data. State any representation of another person. We will only request necessary and proportionate evidence of identity or authority.

We will explain any impediment or clarification needed. Withdrawal does not by itself affect prior lawful processing or legal retention duties.

The studio cannot remotely access saves that exist only on your device. For data controlled by another provider you may also contact that provider. You may complain to the competent data protection authority if you consider your rights were not respected.
