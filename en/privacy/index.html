2. Progress, Google Play Games and optional backup

The game stores progress, Pibbles, worlds and habitats, currency and rewards, inventory, missions, tutorials, songs and records, and language, sound and accessibility preferences on your device. You can play without connecting an account.

Online features are currently in testing and availability depends on the version. In Settings → Help you can connect Google Play Games and separately enable Unity Cloud Save backup. It is unavailable to users under 13 or whose age group is unknown. Connecting Google does not automatically enable backup.

To sign in and recognize the same game, Google and Unity process Google Play Games and Unity Authentication player identifiers, temporary authentication credentials, and technical data needed to provide and protect their services. The game also assigns a random installation identifier to distinguish copies. Your real name, email, photographs, music and personal files are not included in the backup. Google manages your account and Play Games profile under its own terms. Its SDK may process the profile nickname and avatar, plus analytics and diagnostics for service stability and improvements. This version does not request your friends list. Optional Pibble Dance rankings are described below.

If you enable backup, we send your game state to Unity: worlds and boards, discoveries, earned resources, inventory, missions, tutorials, unlocked songs and difficulty-specific records. Account and installation identifiers, dates and synchronization references support recovery and detection of changes across devices. Authorized personnel of the controller can access these records through the project's tools to resolve support incidents and privacy requests; the data is not public to other players.

Backup updates periodically while connected. Recovery restores the last confirmed copy; later actions are not guaranteed if the app closes before synchronization. Before uninstalling or changing phones, select Back up this game and wait for confirmation. You can turn off automatic backup without deleting existing copies. Device preferences are not included in the online copy.

These data support authentication, game continuity, recovery, conflict resolution, security and requests from players. We do not use them for personalized advertising. Public Pibble Dance leaderboards and real purchases are not enabled yet.

Optional Pibble Dance rankings

Rankings are currently limited to private tests and depend on the installed version. Connecting Google Play Games does not publish results. A separate in-game choice lets you share your Play Games nickname adapted to Unity Player Names, best integer score, accuracy and Full Combo mark with participating players, separately for each song and difficulty, using Unity Leaderboards and Cloud Code.

After you choose to participate, newly completed games are submitted automatically. Only a higher score replaces your record for that song and difficulty. The nickname is managed in the Unity Authentication player profile; spaces may be adapted and Unity adds a suffix to distinguish players. It is not stored as custom score metadata. Deleting ranking entries preserves the identity profile; you may request complete identity deletion through support.

To operate and protect rankings, Unity also processes your player identifier, sharing choice, chart version, session dates and ordered note judgements. Cloud Code checks session duration and recalculates the score; this is not a guarantee against every form of cheating. Existing local records and recovered backups are not automatically published. Failed or offline submissions may be queued locally for a later retry.

From the song ranking screen choose Stop sharing and delete scores to stop uploads and request deletion of your online entries, including older chart editions. Wait for confirmation; an offline or failed deletion remains pending. Local progress and records are preserved. A small technical consent/deletion record may remain while the online identity exists to prevent delayed submissions from restoring scores. You may request its removal together with the Unity identity through support. Deleting cloud game backups does not delete ranking entries, and deleting ranking entries does not delete backups.

Scores remain until you request deletion or we retire the relevant ranking edition. Operational logs and provider backups are subject to provider retention obligations and are not normal public ranking entries. Authorized personnel may access relevant service records for support, security and privacy requests.

3. Age and protection of minors

The game is intended for people aged 13 and over. At startup you can enter your age or choose not to answer. The entered age is used to determine a band —under 13, 13–17, or 18 and over— and is then discarded. Only the band or decision not to answer is kept on the device; no date of birth is stored.

When ads are available, for players aged 13–17 we request non-personalized advertising, no sale or sharing for targeted advertising, and restrictions on content and identifiers. For adults, the relevant privacy choices apply. Players under 13 and those whose age is unknown cannot use ads or online sign-in. Public ads remain disabled during the current private tests; advertising trials are limited to authorized adult testers.

A parent or guardian may contact us about a minor's information under our control. Children do not have to send contact details to play. Entering an adult age does not grant advertising consent.

4. Ads and privacy choices

The game integrates Unity LevelPlay and the ironSource, Unity Ads and Google AdMob advertising networks. When available, rewarded ads are optional and may grant the stated benefit upon completion. Availability depends on the service configuration and permitted audience.

Advertising providers may process IP addresses and approximate location derived from them, device or app characteristics and identifiers, ad interactions, and performance and diagnostic data. Purposes include ad delivery and measurement, service operation, security and fraud prevention. Personalized advertising, when available, depends on applicable choices and conditions; an adult age selection alone does not grant permission.

We use Google User Messaging Platform to manage privacy choices where required. Where applicable the game displays a form and provides Privacy options in Settings to review your choices. You can also adjust Android advertising settings. Declining personalization does not prevent local play, but may affect the type or availability of ads.

5. Mission time and support

The game makes an HTTPS request to TimeAPI to check the date for daily missions. Our request does not include game progress, an email or a player identifier. The provider may receive an IP address and technical request data as part of the connection.

When you email support, we receive your email address and any messages or files you choose to send. We use these to answer and resolve your support or privacy request, not for marketing. Do not send passwords, access codes or information unrelated to your request.

7. Retention and deletion

Request deletion of your Pibble Evolution online account

Email lathortylla@gmail.com with the subject Delete my online account — Pibble Evolution. Specify whether you want the entire online identity and associated data removed or only backups, Pibble Dance entries or support messages. Settings → Help → Request online data deletion opens this procedure in builds that include the button.

You can make a request without installing or signing in to the app. We only ask for necessary, proportionate information to locate records and verify that they belong to you. A public identifier or nickname alone does not authorize deletion. Include a support reference from the game if you have one; never send passwords, Google codes, purchase tokens or card numbers.

A full request covers the Unity identity and link, backups, online name and scores and associated purchase data under our control, subject to the retention exceptions below. Before completion we explain its effects on recovery, remaining balance and pass access. It does not delete your Google account, Google's order history or data held only on your phone. Account deletion does not request a Google Play refund.

Delete backups only

Open Settings → Help → Cloud backup → Delete Unity backups and confirm. Automatic uploads stop and deletion of game backups is requested. Wait for confirmation: an offline or failed request stays pending and is retried.

This keeps the local game, online identity, purchases and Pibble Dance scores. Compatible installations stop uploading when they detect deletion after reconnecting. Creating a new backup requires an explicit choice. A technical marker containing the identity and a random reference, without game progress, prevents old copies from returning.

Stop sharing scores

Pibble Dance leaderboards let you stop sharing and request removal of online scores. Local records, backups and the identity profile remain; a minimal record respects the withdrawal. This is separate from full online account deletion.

The local game remains available. Starting over in Settings stops automatic backup and retains existing cloud copies; purchase entitlements are verified separately with the server. Android storage controls or uninstalling remove local files, not remote records.

Backups remain while you use the feature and until you request deletion. Superseded copies are retired after a valid successor is confirmed; different unresolved games remain until you choose which to continue. The marker without progress remains while the identity exists or is needed to respect deletion; you can request its removal with the identity.

The online identity and profile remain until you request deletion. Online scores remain until you request their removal or delete the account. Purchase, spending and delivery records remain while the account and its benefits require recovery, verification or prevention of duplicate delivery. A full deletion request includes this technical ledger under our control. If an applicable obligation, pending refund or dispute, or a specific security or fraud-prevention need requires retention, we limit it to necessary data and explain what remains, why and the applicable period before closing the request. Once that reason ends, the data is deleted or effectively anonymized. A linkable hash is not anonymous merely because it is a hash.

Support and privacy messages and attachments remain for up to 12 months after resolving the inquiry, except for legal obligations or disputes, and are then removed from support systems. This period covers support correspondence, not all purchase records.

Deleting our database records does not automatically erase records held by a provider for its own purposes or its internal backups. For Unity IAP data processed on behalf of the game, we receive and verify your request and instruct Unity to delete it through its official channel. For data Google or another provider handles as a controller, we direct you to its controls or privacy channels in section 6. Providers' retention duties and periods may differ from ours.

9. Purchases, refunds and security

Public real-money sales remain disabled. Private purchase tests use authorized accounts and Google Play test payment methods; they create transaction and entitlement records even without a real charge. This section describes the existing integration and will be reviewed before public sales open.

Google Play processes payments. Unity IAP and our Unity Cloud Code server verify the product, status and connection to the signed-in player. We process the player identifier and its transformed reference, product and purchase token, dates, test indicator, purchased balance, pass duration and status, spending, deliveries, gifts and refunds or revocations. The token is stored with restricted server access for later verification; it is not published on leaderboards or displayed in normal game messages. We do not receive your full card number or security code.

The server stores the remaining purchased balance and deliveries so they can be restored under the same identity without duplicates. This is verified separately from progress backup. The pass is a one-time payment for 14 days, without automatic renewal. Pending purchases deliver nothing until confirmed. Refunded or revoked purchases lose their corresponding benefits: if their Treats have already been spent, an adjustment may remain in the purchased balance; earned Treats and game progress are not deducted by that adjustment. Periodic checks need a connection and confirmation may take time to appear.

Unity IAP and other integrated SDKs may also process installation and session identifiers, technical device information, country, transactions and diagnostic and performance data to provide, measure and protect their services. We have not enabled our own behavioral analytics through Unity Analytics; this does not mean providers collect no operational telemetry.

Time checks, authentication, cloud, leaderboards and purchase communications use HTTPS/TLS. Server access is restricted and private server credentials are not placed in the APK. We do not claim that local save files are encrypted or that security is absolute.

Changes to this notice will be published here with their date. If a new feature requires additional information or consent, we provide it before the relevant processing.
