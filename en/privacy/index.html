2. Progress, Google Play Games and optional backup

The game stores progress, Pibbles, worlds and habitats, currency and rewards, inventory, missions, tutorials, songs and records, and language, sound and accessibility preferences on your device. You can play without connecting an account.

Online features are currently in testing and availability depends on the version. In Settings → Help you can connect Google Play Games and separately enable Unity Cloud Save backup. It is unavailable to users under 13 or whose age group is unknown. Connecting Google does not automatically enable backup.

To sign in and recognize the same game, Google and Unity process Google Play Games and Unity Authentication player identifiers, temporary authentication credentials, and technical data needed to provide and protect their services. The game also assigns a random installation identifier to distinguish copies. Your real name, email, photographs, music and personal files are not included in the backup. Google manages your account and Play Games profile under its own terms. Its SDK may process the profile nickname and avatar, plus analytics and diagnostics for service stability and improvements. This version does not request your friends list or publish a player leaderboard.

If you enable backup, we send your game state to Unity: worlds and boards, discoveries, earned resources, inventory, missions, tutorials, unlocked songs and difficulty-specific records. Account and installation identifiers, dates and synchronization references support recovery and detection of changes across devices. Authorized personnel of the controller can access these records through the project's tools to resolve support incidents and privacy requests; the data is not public to other players.

Backup updates periodically while connected. Recovery restores the last confirmed copy; later actions are not guaranteed if the app closes before synchronization. Before uninstalling or changing phones, select Back up this game and wait for confirmation. You can turn off automatic backup without deleting existing copies. Device preferences are not included in the online copy.

These data support authentication, game continuity, recovery, conflict resolution, security and requests from players. We do not use them for personalized advertising. Public Pibble Dance leaderboards and real purchases are not enabled yet.

3. Age and protection of children

The game is intended for ages 13 and up, including teens and adults. At the start you may enter your age or choose not to answer. The entered age is used only to determine an age group—under 13, 13–17 or 18 and over—and is then discarded. Only the age group or decision not to answer is saved on the device; we do not keep a date of birth.

In this version ads remain disabled for anyone under 18 or whose age is unknown. Entering an age is not consent to advertising and does not replace any required privacy choice.

A parent or guardian may contact us about information concerning their child that is under our control. Children do not need to send us contact details to play.

4. Ads and privacy choices

The game integrates Unity LevelPlay and the ironSource, Unity Ads and Google AdMob advertising networks. When available, rewarded ads are optional and may grant the stated benefit upon completion. Availability depends on the service configuration and permitted audience.

Advertising providers may process IP addresses and approximate location derived from them, device or app characteristics and identifiers, ad interactions, and performance and diagnostic data. Purposes include ad delivery and measurement, service operation, security and fraud prevention. Personalized advertising, when available, depends on applicable choices and conditions; an adult age selection alone does not grant permission.

We use Google User Messaging Platform to manage privacy choices where required. Where applicable the game displays a form and provides Privacy options in Settings to review your choices. You can also adjust Android advertising settings. Declining personalization does not prevent local play, but may affect the type or availability of ads.

5. Mission time and support

The game makes an HTTPS request to TimeAPI to check the date for daily missions. Our request does not include game progress, an email or a player identifier. The provider may receive an IP address and technical request data as part of the connection.

When you email support, we receive your email address and any messages or files you choose to send. We use these to answer and resolve your support or privacy request, not for marketing. Do not send passwords, access codes or information unrelated to your request.

7. Retention and deletion

Delete Unity backups

In a version with this feature, open Settings → Help → Cloud backup → Delete Unity backups and confirm. Automatic uploads stop and deletion of game backups for that identity is requested. Wait for the confirmation message: if offline or an error occurs, the request stays pending and is retried; it is not reported as completed.

Deleting backups keeps this phone's local game and does not delete your Google account. Other compatible installations stop automatic uploads when they detect the deletion after reconnecting. Creating a new backup afterwards requires explicit opt-in again. A technical marker containing the Unity identity and a random deletion reference, without coins, Pibbles or records, is kept to prevent old copies from reappearing.

Delete the online identity or other data under our control

Write to lathortylla@gmail.com with the subject Delete my data — Pibble Evolution and specify whether you request deletion of backups, the Unity identity and link, or support messages. After deleting backups, Copy support reference helps locate the identity and verify the request. Never send passwords or Google codes.

You can request deletion without installing or signing in to the app. We request only necessary and proportionate information to locate the records and establish your relationship with them. Removing a Unity identity also requires addressing its associated service data; deleting an Authentication player alone is not sufficient. We will explain the outcome or any retention required by law or a dispute. This process does not delete your Google account or Play Games profile and does not cancel subscriptions.

Local progress and preferences stay on the device to continue playing. Starting over in Settings stops automatic backup and retains existing cloud copies; it may also retain an active subscription. Use Android storage controls or uninstall to remove all local storage. These actions are separate from remote deletion.

Unity game backups remain while you use the feature and until you request deletion. After a valid successor is confirmed, superseded copies are retired; different unresolved games are kept until you choose which one to continue with. The marker without game progress remains while the online identity exists or is needed to respect deletion; you can request its removal together with the identity through support. Providers may retain operational records and backups under their obligations and policies, outside the game's normal recovery process.

We retain support and privacy messages and attachments for up to 12 months after resolving the inquiry, except for legal obligations or disputes. They are then removed from support systems. Advertising providers' own records are handled through their privacy channels linked in section 6; we can help identify the provider.
