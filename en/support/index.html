Cloud progress and deletion

To delete online copies only, use Settings → Help → Cloud backup → Delete Unity backups and wait for confirmation. Your phone game stays available. To request removal of the Unity identity too, email lathortylla@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my data — Pibble Evolution” and the reference you can copy after deletion. Never send passwords or codes. Requests are also accepted without the app installed; we verify your relationship with the records proportionately.

See the procedure, retention and your rights.

Delete Pibble Dance ranking entries

In a version with rankings, open the song ranking screen and choose Stop sharing and delete scores. Wait for confirmation. This preserves local records and game backups. For removal of the entire Unity identity or help without the app, email lathortylla@gmail.com. See ranking data and deletion.
